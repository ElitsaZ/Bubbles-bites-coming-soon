Privacy Policy

We only collect the details we need to deliver your boxes, keep you informed and run our business, and we never sell your data. This policy explains what we collect, why, and what you can ask of us.

Last updated · 12 November 2026

1. Who we are

Bubbles & Bites is a trade name of Bubbles and Bites BV, located in Haarlem, the Netherlands. KvK number 42083072, VAT number NL869629190B01.

We are the controller of your personal data, which means we decide how and why it is used. For any privacy question, email us at info@bubbles-bites.com or call 06 4458 7322. We aim to reply within 24 hours.

2. What data we collect

We collect data that you give us when you order, sign up or contact us:

  • Contact details: first and last name, email address and phone number.
  • Delivery details: delivery address, chosen delivery day and any delivery notes you add.
  • Order details: which box or package you chose, your order history, pauses, skips and cancellations.
  • Payment details: payment status, payment method and, for packages, the SEPA direct debit or card authorisation. Your full bank or card details are handled by Mollie; we never see or store your full card number.
  • Age confirmation: your confirmation at checkout that you are 18 or older. At the door we look at your ID, but we never copy, photograph or store it. When you first visit our website, we ask for your date of birth; this check happens in your browser only, and we never receive or store your date of birth.
  • Communication: emails, messages and complaints you send us.
  • Marketing preferences: whether you agreed to receive news and offers.
  • Waiting list: your name, email address and postcode when you join our waiting list, and your consent to be kept informed.
  • Website data: technical data such as your IP address and browser type, which our hosting provider needs to show you the website, and, only if you accept analytics cookies, how you use our website (see section 6).

6. Cookies and analytics

We use as few cookies as possible, and we never track you for advertising.

  • Analytics cookies (only with your consent). If you click Accept in our cookie banner, we use Google Analytics to see how visitors find and use our website, for example which pages are visited and where visitors come from. Google Analytics places cookies whose names start with _ga. We have switched off advertising features and data sharing for advertising, and Google does not store full IP addresses. If you click Decline, no analytics cookies are placed.
  • Local storage. To make the website work, your browser remembers three things: that you passed the age check (for 30 days), which language you chose, and your cookie choice. This stays on your own device and is not sent to us. You can delete it at any time in your browser settings.
  • Hosting. Like every website, our hosting provider Vercel processes your IP address and browser details to show you the pages and protect the site against misuse.
  • Waiting list form. When you join the waiting list, your details are sent to Jotform, which processes them for us (see section 4).

You can change your choice at any time via Cookie settings at the bottom of every page, or by deleting cookies in your browser. If you withdraw your consent, we stop Google Analytics and remove its cookies. We do not use marketing cookies; if we ever do, we will update this policy and ask for your consent first.

7. Your rights

You are always in control of your data. You have the right to:

  • See which personal data we hold about you.
  • Correct data that is wrong or incomplete. Many details you can change yourself in your account.
  • Delete your data, unless we must keep it by law (for example invoices).
  • Restrict how we use your data while a question or complaint is being handled.
  • Receive your data in a common format, or have us send it to another company.
  • Object to our use of your data based on our legitimate interest.
  • Withdraw your consent for news and offers at any time, using the unsubscribe link in every email or by contacting us, and for analytics cookies via Cookie settings.

To use any of these rights, email info@bubbles-bites.com. We may ask you to confirm your identity first. We respond within one month.

Not happy with how we handle your data? Please tell us first, so we can put it right. You also have the right to file a complaint with the Dutch Data Protection Authority (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens).

8. Security, age and changes

Security. We protect your data with appropriate measures, such as secure (HTTPS) connections, strong passwords with two-factor authentication on our business accounts, and access limited to the people who need it. If a data breach occurs that puts your data at risk, we will inform you and the authorities as required by law.

Age. Our products contain alcohol, so we do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If we find out that we have, we delete it.

Changes. We may update this policy, for example when we add a new service provider. The latest version is always on our website. If a change is important, we will let you know by email.
